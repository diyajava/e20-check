E20 Check

Ethanol-petrol research project · India

Two tools from a student research project on ethanol-petrol blends. Check whether a vehicle is likely to be E20-ready, and see which blend a climate suggests. This is a research tool built on public statements, not manufacturer advice.

Your vehicle

Year of manufacture, not purchase. E20-compliant production began in April 2023, so the month matters for 2023 vehicles.

Local climate

Enter typical values for a region and season. No city data is included, so look up your own source.
A car only gets what the pump dispenses. This tool is a policy framework for choosing regional or seasonal mandates. It is conceptual and not validated against measured engine data.
Sources behind the checker. Das (2025), Business Standard: industry statements from Tata Motors, Mahindra and Hyundai. Mody (2025), Autocar India: statements from Honda, Bajaj, TVS, Hero and Royal Enfield. Where no statement was found, the checker says so. Always confirm in your owner's manual or with the manufacturer.
